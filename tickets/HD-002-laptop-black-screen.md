# HD-002: Laptop Screen Black with Blinking Power Light

## Ticket Information
- **Ticket ID:** HD-002
- **Reported by:** Michael Brown
- **Department:** Engineering
- **Location:** Floor 1
- **Category:** Hardware Troubleshooting
- **Environment:** ServiceDesk Simulator
- **Status:** Resolved in the simulator

## Reported Issue
Michael Brown reported that his laptop would not start properly. The screen remained black, and the power light slowly blinked. Pressing the power button several times did not solve the problem.

The user needed the laptop working before a 10:00 AM meeting.

## Business Impact
The user could not work until laptop access was restored. This affected his ability to complete work and prepare for his meeting.

## Initial Troubleshooting
The user had already:
- Pressed the power button several times.
- Confirmed that the laptop was plugged in.
- Observed that the power light continued blinking while the screen stayed black.

These steps did not resolve the issue.

## Troubleshooting and Resolution Steps
1. Recognized that a slowly blinking power LED indicated the laptop was likely asleep rather than completely powered off.
2. Contacted the user through Company Chat and instructed him to disconnect the dock, charger, external monitors, and other connected peripherals.
3. Provided detailed instructions for performing a hard reset.
4. Instructed the user to hold the power button for a full 30 seconds, until all lights and the fan stopped.
5. Instructed the user to press the power button once to turn the laptop back on.
6. Confirmed the outcome described by the simulator: the hard reset cleared the stuck sleep state, and the laptop powered back on.

## Resolution
The laptop successfully powered back on after the hard reset. The issue was resolved in the ServiceDesk Simulator.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Laptop power and startup troubleshooting
- Recognizing sleep-mode indicators
- Disconnecting peripherals to isolate an issue
- Performing a hard reset
- Providing remote technical support through chat
- Communicating clear, step-by-step instructions
- Documenting business impact and resolution

## Lessons Learned
A black screen does not always mean a laptop has failed. A slowly blinking power light can indicate that the laptop is stuck in sleep mode. Identifying the symptoms and guiding the user through the correct steps can restore access without immediately assuming that hardware replacement is necessary.

This scenario also demonstrated the importance of explaining each troubleshooting step clearly instead of simply telling the user to perform a hard reset.

## Practice Environment
